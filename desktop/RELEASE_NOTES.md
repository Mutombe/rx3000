The dispensary, rebuilt around the script.

**The script is a table, and it is the biggest thing on the screen**

One row per medicine under column headings — quantity, directions and
amount, readable without opening anything. The table is ruled down to
eight rows whether the script is full or empty, so you can see where
the next line goes.

**Edit and Delete on every row**

Edit opens that line in a dialog with room to read it. Delete takes
the line off the script. Adding a medicine opens its dialog, because a
new line has no directions yet.

**The function keys are back, on the numbers you already know**

Along the foot of the window, where they do not move:

  F1 Mix   F2 Find patient   F3 Add medicine   F4 Diagnosis
  F5 WayBill   F6 Auth   F8 Repts   F9 Hist   F12 Finish

**A bar that is only what you can do**

Route tabs on the left; New script, New quote, Alter script and
History on the right, with their names back.

**One screen**

Patient and prescriber share a row. Where a screen is genuinely too
short the page scrolls rather than hiding what did not fit — which is
what was cutting the table off entirely two releases ago.
