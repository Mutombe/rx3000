Two things on the dispensary screen, both reported from a counter.

**The stage bars across the top are gone**

Choosing a medicine used to put a row of chips above the script saying which
part of the job you were on. They named the sections that are already named,
in the same words, twelve pixels lower down. They also counted wrongly: on an
ordinary prescription two of them carried the same number, so the screen was
telling a dispenser something untrue about where they were.

Nothing is lost with them. The headings carry the order. The line beside the
Finish button carries what is still missing, which is what anybody looks at
when the button will not go.

**The three figures on the worklist can be read again**

The three counts at the top of the rail had been squashed to a sliver, so all
you could see were the tops of three buttons under the WORKLIST heading. They were the right size all along and the panel was taking
their height for the list below. They are full height now, and the rail draws
them as three plain tiles instead of three boxes inside a box.
